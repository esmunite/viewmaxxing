Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026. Short version: we collect the minimum needed to run the app, we don't sell your data, and there are no ads or tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Account info — your email address when you sign up (via Google or email/password).
  • Videos you upload — to grade them. Your video is processed in your browser and sent to our grading service, where AI models (Google Gemini and Groq) analyze the visuals, audio, and transcript.
  • Your Creative Brief answers — goal, platforms, intended viewer, and any optional details you add.
  • Your grades — the scores and feedback we generate, saved to your history.

What we don't collect

  • No tracking cookies, no analytics beacons, no advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase — stores your account and grades (authentication + database).
  • Vercel — hosts the website.
  • Google (Gemini) and Groq — AI models that grade your video. Your video content is sent to them only for grading.

Your control

Your videos are only used to produce your grades — never to train models for anyone else, and never shown to other users. Want your account and data deleted? Contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.
