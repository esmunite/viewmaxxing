How Scoring Works

How grading works

More Than a Score. A Clearer Direction.

BennettIQ evaluates your video against your intended goal, helping you identify what's working, what could improve, and what to change before you publish.

Virality

Get watched, shared, and rewatched. Opening seconds, emotion, and shareability carry the most weight.

Conversion

Move a viewer to take action. Offer clarity, proof, and one clear call to action matter most.

Growth

Earn followers from the right audience. Real value today, and a reason to come back tomorrow.

Engagement

Earn comments, saves, and shares. Emotion and discussion potential lead the rubric.

What the score means

Your score is our AI's read of your video against your goal's rubric, out of 100. It is not a prediction. An 80 is not an 80 percent chance of anything, and a high score never guarantees views, virality, or sales.

90–100Exceptional
80–89Strong
70–79Promising
60–69Mixed
40–59Weak

Privacy

Your Content Deserves Transparency.

Understand how your videos are processed and what happens to your content when you use BennettIQ. We believe creators should have clear answers before uploading their work.

How your videos are processed

Frames and audio are pulled from your video right in the browser. The audio is transcribed, then AI models study the visuals, the transcript, and your brief to build your grade. Grading runs on Google Gemini, with backup models standing by if it is ever down.

Your content stays yours

You keep full ownership of everything you upload. Videos are used only to produce your grades. They never train models for anyone else and are never shown to other users. No tracking cookies, no ads.

Your data, your call

Grades live in your private history. You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
