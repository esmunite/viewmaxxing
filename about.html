I'm Elijah Moua, a finance student and builder based in Wisconsin.

I'm a junior at UW-Whitewater studying finance. BennettIQ started with a frustration I kept running into: I would finish a video, stare at it, and have no honest way to know whether it was actually good before posting it. So I built the tool I wanted. One that watches your video the way a viewer would, tells you the truth about it, and shows its work. No guru talk, no fake promises. Just a straight grade and the fixes that matter.

What BennettIQ does

Upload a video, tell us where it'll be posted and what you're trying to achieve, and BennettIQ grades it: one score out of 100, backed by timestamps, quotes, and frames from your actual footage. Every dimension comes with what's working, what's hurting, and one concrete fix.

Three free grades a day. No credit card required.
