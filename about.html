About.

I'm Elijah Moua, and I created BennettIQ from my parents basement.

At the ripe age of 22 years old and multiple, multiple failed business attempts, I decided to go “all in” on BennettIQ because I was once and still are in your shoes, wanting to better my content.

You’ve probably seen me and BennettIQ on social media, so I want to personally say thank you for supporting this project. Everyday I’m finding ways to improve the user experience, user interface, and the backend/engineering of the app to create a better and productive tool for you.

What BennettIQ does

Upload a video, tell us where it'll be posted and what you're trying to achieve, and BennettIQ grades it: one score out of 100, backed by timestamps, quotes, and frames from your actual footage. Every dimension comes with what's working, what's hurting, and one concrete fix.

Three free grades a day. No credit card required.
