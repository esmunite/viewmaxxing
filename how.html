How grading works

Every video gets one score out of 100, weighted for the goal you picked: virality, conversion, growth, or engagement. Same video, different goal, different score.

One score, weighted for your goal

Each goal is graded on its own weighted dimensions. A video chasing virality is judged heavily on its opening seconds and pacing; one chasing conversion is judged on its offer, proof, and call to action. The breakdown shows every dimension with its weight, plus what's working, what's hurting, and one concrete fix, each tied to a timestamp, quote, or frame from your actual footage.

The bands

ScoreMeaning
90–100Exceptional: among the best in a competitive feed for this goal
80–89Strong: minor polish left
70–79Promising: meaningful gaps to fix
60–69Mixed: several fixes would help a lot
40–59Weak for this goal: substantial rework needed
0–39Fundamentally misaligned: rethink the concept

The scores are deliberately tough. A 90 should feel earned.

Hook frameworks BennettIQ looks for

Your opening is graded against seven proven archetypes: pattern interrupt, open loop, bold or negative claim, numbered promise, direct question, in-medias-res, and relatable POV. Your fixes name the exact framework to use, with a concrete rewrite, not generic advice.

Honest limits

  • No virality predictions. We don't have historical performance data, so BennettIQ won't guess your view count. Anyone who claims to predict that is guessing.
  • 3 free grades per day, resetting at midnight UTC.
  • Visual analysis watches your actual video frames. If the vision model is ever down, you'll see a notice and get a transcript-only grade instead. It'll say so, not pretend.
